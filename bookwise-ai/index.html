Questions

Is this tax advice?

No. Bookwise gives educational estimates from 2026 federal rules and a flat state rate. Use it to find what to ask about, then confirm eligibility and filing with a CPA or enrolled agent.

Which tax year does it use?

2026: IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32 and Notice 2025-67, plus the One Big Beautiful Bill Act changes to QBI, bonus depreciation, §179, the SALT cap and the childcare credit. Mileage reflects the July 1 rise to 76 cents.

How is the S-corp salary picked?

You set a minimum reasonable salary, or we estimate one for your industry. The engine tests every salary from that floor up to your full profit and keeps the one with the lowest total tax.

Where is my data stored?

In this version, your account and books stay in your own browser and are not sent to our servers. Settings has a backup download for moving between devices.

Can my accountant see it?

Print or save any report and the tax plan as a PDF, and export transactions as CSV with Schedule C line numbers.

Can I cancel?

Starter is free for good. Paid plans can be changed or cancelled from your account page at any time.
