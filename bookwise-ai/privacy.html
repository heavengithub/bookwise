Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Template — have counsel review before launch.

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, business name and the business and household figures you choose to enter. Passwords are stored only as salted PBKDF2 hashes.

Where your data lives

In the current version of Bookwise, your account and all of your books, invoices and tax profile are stored in your own web browser on your device (browser local storage). They are not transmitted to or stored on our servers. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so use Settings → Download backup regularly.

Contact form

If you use the contact form on our website, the name, email and message you submit are processed by our hosting provider (Netlify) and delivered to us so we can respond.

How we use information

  • To provide and improve the service
  • To respond to your messages
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, export your data, or permanently delete your account at any time from Account & security.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the contact form on our homepage.
